## Vision-language model robustness

**Physical Superintelligence Lab, University of Southern California**  
Research Assistant · June 2025–Present

I investigate how vision-language models fail and how targeted post-training can improve their robustness and reliability. My work includes:

- Developing pipelines to generate, filter, and evaluate multimodal data across visual reasoning tasks.
- Analyzing failures in object recognition, attributes, spatial relationships, counting, and negation.
- Applying large vision-language models and parameter-efficient fine-tuning to study targeted repair and generalization.

Related work is listed on my [publications page]({{ '/publications/' | relative_url }}), including _Large Reward Models_ and _FuzzingRL_.

## Microfabrication and quantum transport

**Laboratory of Microfabrication, Institute of Physics, Chinese Academy of Sciences**  
Research Assistant · May–August 2023

I assisted with microelectronic substrate fabrication for terahertz communication devices using lift-off processes. I operated mask aligners, ICPCVD systems, and electron-beam evaporation tools, and supported ultra-low-temperature experiments and quantum transport measurements.
